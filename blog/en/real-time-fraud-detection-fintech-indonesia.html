Designing Sub-50ms Payment Anomaly & Fraud Detection for Indonesian Fintech

Albertus Freddi 2026-10-19 12 min read
Read in: EN ID 中文 日本語 한국어

Detecting account takeovers, QRIS manipulation, and high-velocity fund draining using streaming Redis graphs and rule engines.

The Rise of QRIS Tampering and Account Takeover (ATO)

As QRIS adoption skyrocketed across merchants in Indonesia, organized fraud rings began deploying subtle attack vectors: spoofed merchant dynamic payloads, fast account takeovers, and micro-transaction card testing.

The Sub-50ms Latency Budget

Payment gateway switches require fraud risk scores to be returned within 50 milliseconds to prevent checkout dropoffs. Complex multi-layer deep neural networks are too slow for inline transactional evaluation.

High-Velocity Sliding Windows in Redis

We structured velocity counters using Redis Sorted Sets and Bloom filters. Tracking IP velocity, device fingerprint shifts, and rapid disbursement triggers catches 92% of automated credential stuffing attacks before money leaves the escrow account.

Automated Risk Tiering and Step-Up Verification

Rather than hard-rejecting ambiguous transactions, the system initiates step-up biometric or WhatsApp OTP verification, keeping false positive transaction friction below 0.4%.
