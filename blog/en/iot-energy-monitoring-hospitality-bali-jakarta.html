The Cost of Unregulated Air Conditioning in Tropical Resorts

Air conditioning represents over 65% of hotel electricity bills in tropical destinations like Bali. Guests frequently leave balcony doors wide open or keep AC units running at 16°C while away on day tours.

ESP32 & Zigbee Environmental Telemetry

We deployed non-invasive door contact sensors and PIR presence detectors connected to low-power ESP32 microcontrollers that bridge over MQTT to a centralized property automation controller.

Dynamic Setback Algorithms

When a room remains unoccupied for over 35 minutes or patio doors are detected open, the automation system adjusts AC setpoints to an energy-saving 25°C curve without shutting the compressor down abruptly.
