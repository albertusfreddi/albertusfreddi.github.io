The Failure Mode of Standard Vector Embeddings

Pre-trained models like OpenAI text-embedding-3 perform well on formal Indonesian news articles, but collapse when handling conversational inputs like "min paket sy blm smpe pdhl udh tf kmrn". Vector cosine similarity scores plummet, causing knowledge retrieval to miss relevant policies.

The Colloquial Slang Normalization Dictionary

Before text is passed to the embedding model or RAG retriever, we pipe inbound customer messages through a compiled finite-state dictionary mapping over 4,500 Indonesian slang contractions, phonetic typos, and regional suffixes back to standard root lemmas.

Hybrid Dense-Sparse Retrieval Architecture

Combining dense embeddings with BM25 sparse keyword matching guarantees that specific order alphanumeric codes, bank transfer numbers, and exact product SKUs are never lost in vector latent space.

Deterministic Guardrails on Policy and Pricing

AI models must never invent refund amounts or warranty terms. Strict system prompt boundary envelopes and validator functions intercept output tokens before they are transmitted over the live customer session.
