The Island of Disconnected Kitchen Systems

In many casual dining chains in Jakarta, kitchen staff print tickets from offline desktop POS systems while raw ingredient inventory is recorded manually at night on paper clipboards. Discrepancies between sold items and stock wastage averaged 14%.

Raw ESC/POS Byte Interception on the LAN

Instead of ripping out existing hardware, we deployed lightweight Raspberry Pi print spoolers on the local network. By reading raw ESC/POS command bytes sent to Epson thermal printers, the agent decodes order tickets in real-time.

Real-Time Bill of Materials (BOM) Depletion

Every time a tenderized steak or coffee order prints in the kitchen, the exact gram-level recipe BOM is deducted from central Postgres inventory tables via encrypted WebSocket transactions.
