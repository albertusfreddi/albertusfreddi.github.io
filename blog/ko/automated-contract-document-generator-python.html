The Friction in Enterprise Sales Closing

Closing B2B contracts often stalls when legal and sales ops spend days manually copy-pasting client company details, tax registration numbers (NPWP), and fee schedules into Word templates.

Headless Word Template Compilation with Python-docx

We created an automated pipeline that takes structured CRM deal webhooks, validates corporate registry numbers against official tax formats, and compiles pixel-perfect Word and PDF documents with dynamic schedules and conditional indemnity clauses.

Indonesian Certified Digital Signature Compliance (PSrE)

Under Indonesian Ministry of Communication and Informatics (Kominfo) regulations, digital signatures must meet PSrE certification standards to carry evidentiary weight in court. The pipeline automatically registers signing envelopes via certified providers (Privy, VIDA).

Tamper-Evident Document Hashing and Archival

Signed PDF certificates are SHA-256 hashed and deposited into immutable S3 object storage with versioning enabled, automatically pinging client deal Slack channels with download links.
