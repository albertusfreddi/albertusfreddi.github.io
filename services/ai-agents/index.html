What it is

AI Agents &Workflow Orchestration

An agent is not a chatbot. It is a program that reads your data, decides what to do with it, does it, and tells you what happened. It runs on a schedule or on a webhook, and nobody has to sit and watch it.

What I take off your desk

  • Reading incoming messages, scoring them, and replying or routing them to the right person.
  • Moving data between your tools on a schedule so nobody copies and pastes again.
  • Watching a folder, an inbox or a sheet and acting the moment something changes.
  • Summarising long documents, calls or threads into the exact format your team already uses.
